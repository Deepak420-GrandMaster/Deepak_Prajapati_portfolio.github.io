01 / Beyond the CV

How I work.

The habits that show up in everything I build.

  • I check the number first

    Years of quality control taught me not to trust a figure until it has been traced to its source. At TravelJuice that meant following an 82% gap between Meta Ads and GA4 back to consent-gated tracking, and closing it.

  • Evaluation, not demos

    For my travel assistant I wrote test cases for unsupported claims, incomplete retrieval, ambiguous queries and conflicting sources, then let RAGAS decide each iteration. Faithfulness went from 0.60 to 0.92.

  • I own the whole pipeline

    Tracking, warehouse, models and the tools people click: I like being responsible for all of it. Owning the stack is how I found the 70% saving in BigQuery.

  • I teach what I learn

    I have led teams of 10 to 12 analysts and run more than 25 trainings and cross-trainings. If a process only works when I’m in the room, it isn’t finished.
